Welcome to Little Isle 🌤️

A tiny block-built world, all yours to explore. Gather from the island, place blocks, and watch the sun set over your home.

🕹️ Move with the stick or WASD✦ Gather nearby resources🧱 Select a block, then Build🌙 Time flows from day to night

Tip: collect wood, stone, and flowers. Your island is small, but there's plenty to discover.

Island guide 📖

Explore the island to find trees, stone, and wildflowers. Move close and press Gather (E) to collect what you're facing. Choose an inventory slot and press Build (Q) to place a block.

Movement: WASD / arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick. Space / Jump hops over things. The day-night cycle is always ticking.
