HOW TO PLAY

TOUCH / TABLET

◉
Move

Touch and drag anywhere on the lower left. Push further to sprint.

◈
Aim

Drag on the right side of the screen. Crosshair stays centered.

✦
Fight

Hold FIRE (auto-aim assist is on). AIM tightens spread; RELOAD when dry.

▤
Build

Tap BUILD, pick WALL or RAMP, then PLACE. Harvest trees and rocks with your pickaxe.

KEYBOARD / MOUSE

⌨
Move

WASD to move, SHIFT to sprint, SPACE to jump.

🖱
Aim / Fire

Click the island to lock the mouse. Left click shoots, right click aims. R reloads.

▦
Build

B toggles build mode, Q swaps wall/ramp, F or click places.
